# ATLAS Companion for DCS Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 1, 2026

ATLAS Companion for DCS is a read-only companion for ATLAS and Digital Combat Simulator. This policy explains how the app handles information.

## Information the app handles

The app connects only to an ATLAS server selected by the user. To provide live mission awareness, it receives information made available by that server, which may include mission identity, aircraft and carrier positions, airfield and runway information, chart data, and assigned procedure details.

The app stores saved server names and addresses in the device's local preferences. Pairing links and session keys are stored in the device Keychain. This information remains on the user's device unless the user chooses to share it.

## Information we collect

We do not operate an account system and do not collect personal information through the app. The app does not include advertising, analytics, third-party tracking, crash-reporting services, or background location tracking. It does not request microphone, contacts, photos, camera, or precise-location access.

Live ATLAS data is requested directly from the user-selected ATLAS server and is processed on the device. We do not receive or retain that traffic through a developer-operated service.

## Local network access

The app requests Local Network permission so it can connect to an ATLAS server on the user's network. The user controls which server is added and may remove a saved server at any time.

## Data retention and deletion

Saved server details and pairing credentials remain on the device until the user removes the server or uninstalls the app. Because we do not receive this data, there is no separate developer-held copy to delete.

## External links

The app may provide a link to the ATLAS community on Discord. Opening an external service is optional and is governed by that service's own privacy policy.

## Children's privacy

The app is not designed to collect personal information from children, and we do not knowingly collect such information.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app's data practices change. The current version will be published at this URL with its effective date.
